## Overview
**BUSINESS OPERATOR** is a modular, zero-budget-first business engineering and automation system built on GitHub. It transforms operational diagnostics, workflow automation, and market intelligence into repeatable, auditable digital assets and commercial services.

## Core Architecture
The repository is structured as a parametric, modular ecosystem:

```text
BUSINESS-OPERATOR/
├── opportunity-engine/       # Market scanning, pain analysis & opportunity scoring
├── product-factory/          # Prompt packs, audit report templates & workflow blueprints
├── customer-acquisition/     # Ethical outreach frameworks & lead pipeline schemas
├── revenue-intelligence/     # Pilot tracking, SKU performance & operational scorecards
├── knowledge-base/           # Market research, service catalog & pricing hypotheses
├── .github/                  # CI/CD workflows, issue templates & PR governance
├── docs/                     # Static documentation & architecture specs
└── tests/                    # Automated validation & schema checks
